HOW TO PLAY

RULES

Know the rules. Top the leaderboard.

Getting In

A host starts a game from the Host screen and receives a 4-character game PIN. Players join with that PIN, pick a name and an avatar, and wait in the lobby until the host starts the game.

Answering Questions

The challenge has two rounds of 10 questions, played on two different days: Round 1 on day one and Round 2 the next day. Each round mixes easy, intermediate and hard questions. Every question has a countdown timer -- run out of time and it counts as a wrong answer. When you finish Round 1 your score is saved; the answers go up on the big screen. On day two, rejoin with the same name and PIN and the host starts Round 2 for everyone.

Scoring

Everyone plays both rounds — nobody is knocked out. A correct answer is worth 5 points. A wrong answer (or running out of time) scores 0 points.

⚡ Speed bonus: answer correctly fast for up to +3 extra — +3 in the first quarter of the timer, +2 in the second, +1 in the third.

🔥 Streak bonus: every 3 correct answers in a row earns +2 extra. A wrong answer resets your streak.

Lifelines

Each investigator gets two lifelines, usable once each per game: 50/50 removes two incorrect options, and Ask the Team reveals what percentage of other players chose each answer. Each lifeline costs 1 point (your score never goes below zero).

Winning

Once everyone has finished, the investigator with the highest score wins. If scores are level, the most correct answers wins, then whoever finished first.
