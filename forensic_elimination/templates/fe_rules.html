HOW TO PLAY

RULES

Know the rules. Outlast the field.

Getting In

A host starts a game from the Host screen and receives a 4-character game PIN. Players join with that PIN, pick a name and an avatar, and wait in the lobby until the host starts the game.

Answering Questions

Once the game starts, each investigator moves through three rounds at their own pace: Round 1 (Easy), Round 2 (Intermediate), and Round 3 (Hard). Every question has a countdown timer -- run out of time and it counts as a wrong answer.

Elimination

When Elimination is turned on, every investigator starts with three lives. Each wrong answer (or missed timer) costs a life, and losing your third life ends your investigation. When it's off, wrong answers don't cost anything and you keep going.

Lifelines

Each investigator gets two lifelines, usable once each per game: 50/50 removes two incorrect options, and Ask the Team reveals what percentage of other players chose each answer.

Winning

The last investigator standing wins. If more than one investigator survives all three rounds, they're declared joint winners on the Leaderboard.
