HOW TO PLAY

Everything your team needs to know before the investigation begins.

01

Join as a team

Enter a team name and the game code your host gives you. Everyone on your team shares one screen and one investigation.

02

Five rounds, one case

Each round unlocks new evidence — financial records, digital logs, interview transcripts. Read carefully: later rounds depend on details from earlier ones.

03

Beat the clock

Every round has its own timer. Submit your answers before time runs out, or the round closes automatically with whatever you've selected.

04

Score points for accuracy

Each question is worth points toward your team's total. The final round is worth the most — your verdict on who committed the fraud matters most of all.

05

Ties go to a tiebreaker

If teams are tied for first place after Round 5, those teams face one final, harder bonus question with its own 2-minute clock to determine the winner.
