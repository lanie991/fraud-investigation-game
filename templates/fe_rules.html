HOW TO PLAY

RULES

Know the rules. Outlast the field.

Getting In

A host starts a game from the Host screen and receives a 4-character game PIN. Players join with that PIN, pick a name and an avatar, and wait in the lobby until the host starts the game.

Answering Questions

Once the game starts, each investigator moves through their own set of questions at their own pace: four Easy questions, then three Intermediate, then three Hard. Every question has a countdown timer -- run out of time and it counts as a wrong answer.

Elimination

When Elimination is turned on, a single wrong answer (or a missed timer) ends your investigation immediately. When it's off, wrong answers simply earn no points and you keep going.

Lifelines

Each investigator gets one 50/50 lifeline per game, removing two incorrect options from the current question. Use it wisely -- it only works once.

The Final Round

Survive all ten questions and you'll reach the Final Round: a full case file worth 50 points, split across five questions covering the control weakness, the scheme, the evidence, the Fraud Triangle and your final conclusion.

Winning

The last investigator standing -- or the highest score once everyone has finished or been eliminated -- is declared the winner on the Leaderboard.
